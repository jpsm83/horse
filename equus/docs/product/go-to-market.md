# Equus — Go to Market

Beachhead, languages, growth loop, and validation stance. Index: [`businessPlan.md`](businessPlan.md). Interviews: [`validationPlaybook.md`](validationPlaybook.md). Money: [`monetization.md`](monetization.md).

## Beachhead

**First sales and interviews: Spain.**

- Currency for those entities: **EUR** (catalog in [`monetization.md`](monetization.md)).  
- We still **support** USD and GBP **pricing shape** for entities whose location is US/UK; we are **not** running US/UK GTM first.  
- Cluster: real yards in one geography so horse↔stable links are local and chat is useful.

Do not confuse beachhead with UI language.

## Languages (product)

| Locale | Role |
|--------|------|
| **English** | **Primary and default** |
| Spanish | Supported (Spain GTM) |
| Portuguese | Supported |

All three ship at launch. Default for a new user is **English** unless they choose otherwise (`preferredLanguage`).

## Who we sell to first

**Paying customer:** stable owners / managers in Spain (willingness to pay **for yard SaaS**).

**Users we need but do not bill:** horse owners (Hub, chat, portal). Interview them for workflow and transparency, **not** for an Equus card.

Trainers and vets: useful as **collaborators** at launch; they are **not** the production-gate product. Do not sell them a module we have not built.

## Growth loop

```
Yard signs up (catalog + 30 days free)
  → Runs roster / whiteboard / invoices (SaaS)
  → Creates boarded horses or accepts owner invites
  → Emails owners to claim Hub (waiting-transfer nags if stable created the horse)
  → Owners join for free (chat, Hub, portal while yard is in good standing)
  → Graph densifies; later a vet module can attach to the same horses
```

The yard is the **promoter** because the tool is their daily OS, not because they earn 10% of an owner subscription.

Invites by email stay. **No** partner commission program at launch (old Section 19 is dead). Revisit B2B “refer a yard” later if needed.

## Sequencing after launch

1. Hold Spain; make Stable SaaS indispensable (EquineM-parity ops + owner portal).  
2. Add the next **paid module** (veterinary is the natural second SaaS).  
3. Trainer / groomer / others as independent SaaS + workplace collab.  
4. More countries: **own** price lists in local currency; do not silently FX the EUR table.  
5. Marketplace last.

## Competitive GTM story (yards)

“You pay for the stable product, similar to EquineM/HippoVibe. Owners get a modern Hub and chat for free. The horse is not trapped in your tenant — vets and trainers can be real accounts later. If you stop paying, you go read-only; the public page stays.”

Owners: “Your stable invited you. You don’t pay us. You see care and invoices in Hub while the stable’s subscription is active.”

## What we are not pitching

- Free ERP for stables  
- $99/horse owner plans  
- Instagram for horses  
- Instant pan-EU paid acquisition  

## Success (early)

Not vanity signups. **Operationally active Spanish stables** (weekly whiteboard / roster / invoices) and **owners opening Hub/chat** on hosted horses. Metrics: [`metricsSpec.md`](metricsSpec.md).
